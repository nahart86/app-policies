Meet Poploft, a bright, one-thumb puzzle made for your next little break. Tap matching blocks, make satisfying clears, and keep a little breathing room.

♥◆☀❧

Tap. Pop. Repeat.

Tap a connected group of two or more matching blocks. Portrait rounds use 6 × 9; landscape rounds use 9 × 6. The next row rises after two or five ordinary pops, respectively. Peek at what's coming and find your next big clear. Forge and charge five powers, grow your level and make room for a bigger pop. No timer. Take your time.
