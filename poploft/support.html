How to play

Tap two or more matching blocks connected horizontally or vertically. On Apple TV, move with the remote ring and click to pop. Blocks above a clear fall straight down. Bigger groups score more points. The exact previewed row rises after two ordinary pops on a portrait 6 × 9 board or five on a landscape 9 × 6 board. Each round keeps its shape.

Clear five or more blocks to forge the next power into the preview in the column you tap. Do it again in that column before the row rises to charge the power. Explore Laser, Bomb, Stitch, Paint Splash and Frost in Settings → Power workshop. On Apple TV, select Stitch targets, then press Play/Pause or choose Pop selected.

Make it yours

Choose System, Light or Dark appearance in Settings. Phone and tablet also offer sound, haptics, practice and a profile with optional sharing. On iPhone and full-screen iPadOS 26 or later, the Lock control holds the current orientation; smaller or older iPad windows show system-lock guidance. New Game starts a fresh board while keeping your best score and level. A rotation choice lets you keep the current round or start in the new orientation.

If no matches remain, the next row rises early. If a new row would push a block beyond the top of the board, the game ends. Tap Play Again for a fresh board.

Your progress

Poploft saves your current game, best score, level, XP, active playtime, records and preferences on your device. Menus, practice, results, ads and background time do not count as active play. Progress is independent on each device; there is no account or cross-device sync. Your game works offline. Uninstalling the app removes its local progress.

Advertising

The iPhone and iPad version can show occasional full-screen ads between finished games. The Apple TV version has no ads. Ads never appear during an active board. If an ad isn't available, your next game starts immediately.

Report an inappropriate ad

Use the ad's own reporting control if available, or email an ad report. Please include the advertiser name, what was inappropriate, and approximate time. Screenshots are optional; remove personal information before sending.
