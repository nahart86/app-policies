PoploftPrivacy policy

Questions or feedback?

Email nahart86@gmail.com. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, and a description of what happened. Please avoid sending passwords or financial information.

How to play

Tap two or more matching blocks connected horizontally or vertically. Blocks above the clear fall straight down. Bigger groups score more points. Every two successful pops, the previewed row rises from below.

If no matches remain, the next row rises early. If a new row would push a block beyond the top of the board, the game ends. Tap Play Again for a fresh board.

Your progress

Poploft saves your current game, best score, sound preference, and haptic preference on your device. Your game works offline. Uninstalling the app removes its local progress.

Advertising

Occasional full-screen ads can appear between finished games. Ads never appear during an active board. If an ad isn't available, your next game starts immediately.

Report an inappropriate ad

Use the ad's own reporting control if available, or email an ad report. Please include the advertiser name, what was inappropriate, and approximate time. Screenshots are optional; remove personal information before sending.
