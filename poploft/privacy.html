Privacy policy.

Effective October 18, 2026 · Developer: Nathan Hart

PoploftSupport

Information stored on your device

Poploft stores your current board, score, best score, completed-game count, level, XP, active playtime, personal records, and sound, haptic, tutorial and appearance preferences locally on your device. Poploft does not send this game progress to a developer-operated server. The game has no user account or login and does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, or precise location.

Advertising and third parties

The iPhone and iPad version of Poploft includes Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform to support advertising and privacy choices. When advertising is enabled and permitted, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may process your IP address (which can indicate general location), device or app identifiers, advertising and interaction information, crash logs, and performance information. These support ad delivery, measurement, security, and fraud prevention. Non-personalized advertising can still involve data processing.

Poploft requests non-personalized ads, disables Google's publisher first-party ID feature, and does not request permission to access Apple's advertising identifier for tracking. Google and its advertising partners process information under their own policies. Learn more in Google's privacy policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services. Google's SDK disclosure describes its data practices.

The Apple TV version has no ads and does not include Google Mobile Ads or its consent SDK. Each device keeps its own local progress. If you choose Share in the phone or tablet profile, the native share sheet sends only the rank and gameplay record text you select; Poploft does not send it to a developer server.

Your choices

Where required, Google's consent form is presented before ad requests. A Privacy choices control is available in Settings when Google requires it, so you can revisit your choices. If ads cannot be requested or loaded, you can continue playing. You can play offline and can change appearance and sound in Settings, plus haptics on supported devices.

Support communications

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use that information to respond to your request and investigate problems. We retain support correspondence only as needed to resolve requests and maintain relevant records. You can request deletion of support correspondence by contacting us.

Retention and deletion

Local progress remains on your device until app data is removed, such as by uninstalling Poploft. Device backups may retain app data under your Apple settings. Google controls retention of information it processes; consult its policies and privacy controls. Contact us with requests concerning data we hold. We do not sell game-progress information or operate our own user-data brokerage.

Children

Poploft is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can address it.

Website hosting

These pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may receive technical request information, such as IP addresses, when you visit. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Changes and contact

We will update this page and its effective date if our practices change. For privacy requests or questions, email nahart86@gmail.com.
